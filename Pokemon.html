About rate limits

This site works out of the box with no API key. Prices come from the Pokémon TCG index at ~1,000 free requests/day, cached locally. If you still have a legacy pokemontcg.io API key (new registrations are closed; keys work until March 2027), paste it here to raise your limit to 20,000/day.
