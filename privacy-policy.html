Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile applications published by Jeshii Lantz, including Rokuyo and Japan Driving Test Pro.

Information We Collect

Our apps do not collect any personally identifiable information directly. However, we use third-party services that may collect certain data:

  • Advertising ID — Used by Google AdMob to serve personalized or non-personalized ads
  • App Interactions — How you use the app (buttons tapped, screens viewed)
  • Crash Logs — Technical data when the app crashes, to help us fix bugs
  • Device Information — Device model, OS version, and general location (country/region level)

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — For displaying advertisements. AdMob may use your advertising ID to personalize ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services — For app distribution and basic analytics

How We Use Your Data

  • To display relevant advertisements
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To understand how users interact with the app

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the third-party services listed above to provide app functionality and advertising.

Your Rights

  • You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID)
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
  • You can uninstall the app at any time

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Google Play Store listing.
