Child Safety Standards & CSAM Prevention Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Zero Tolerance Policy

Ghost Frequency has a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to keeping our platform safe and actively prohibit any content that exploits or endangers children.

Content Monitoring and Removal

Any user found transmitting, sharing, or promoting CSAM/CSAE over the Ghost Frequency network will be immediately and permanently banned from the platform. The offending content and the user's transmission data will be removed immediately.

Reporting to Law Enforcement

We comply fully with all applicable laws regarding the reporting of CSAM. Any detected instances of CSAM will be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant local law enforcement authorities, along with all available identifying data of the offending user.

User Reporting

If you encounter any suspicious or illegal activity on Ghost Frequency, please report it immediately to our safety compliance officer at jeshiilantz@gmail.com.
